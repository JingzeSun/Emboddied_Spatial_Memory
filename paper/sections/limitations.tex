\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

The primary hypothesis holds only with simulator instance masks, which expose no identity but are close to perfect; with SAM~2.1 only the stale-entity reduction holds.
Identity continuity stays low (about one relocated object in five re-attached even with instance masks), and with SAM~2.1 more than half of the in-scope entities VSMT-lean retracts belong to objects still in place; whether they are later reactivated, and how many are redundant part entities, is not separated in our exports.
The rule-based arms are our adapters of published mechanisms, not the original systems, and several selected configurations lie at the end of their registered grids (Appendix~\ref{app:selection}), which we did not widen after the test.
The target of inference is a training procedure with five seeds; the two-level bound has no coverage guarantee and was calibrated only by a null simulation.
The primary hypothesis was revised, the fixed order designed and identity continuity redefined after development and confirmation readings, though before validation and test; comparisons outside the fixed-order test are descriptive, uncorrected for multiplicity, and partly computed after the test.
The test is simulated; the 3RScan check is descriptive, covers only the instance column and uses proxy truth, a window without frames and offline poses; no real-robot experiment was run.
We do not revise places, relations or topology, and do not learn splitting or merging.
